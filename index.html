What comes over the air

Not a show about the practice. The recordings are the practice. Each broadcast is one real session: someone goes somewhere that doesn't exist and describes what they find, and the tape runs. Nothing is acted.

i · the signal

The hour opens

Two minutes from the Keeper. Not a host introduction. A transmission that establishes where you are and asks you to sit still.

ii · the passage

Somebody goes

Twelve to fifteen minutes cut from a real session. One traveler, one place, their own voice, unperformed. This is the body of the broadcast.

iii · the return

What they carried out

Recorded afterward. The traveler names one thing they brought back. Without this a broadcast is only atmosphere.

Between the parts, the advertisements. They are part of the story and always have been.

Program One — Field Recordings from Nowhere

Thirteen broadcasts. Sundays at ten, Mountain time. When the thirteen are done the program is boxed and the next one takes a different form. A station that never ends is a feed. This one ends on purpose.

One transmitted. Twelve to go. The first broadcast is offered to the next person who plays.

Three ways in

listen

Be there at the hour

Every broadcast asks one question and then leaves real silence after it, long enough that an answer forms whether or not you say it out loud. That silence is the whole participation. Nothing to press.

write in

Tell us what you saw

One address. A voice memo or a few lines. Some are read on the air, in the sender's own words. This is the third category in the tagline, and it is not decorative.

Ask for a passage

Anyone can request a session. Ninety minutes, nothing to prepare, nothing to get right. Some become broadcasts, and only ever with your say-so after you have heard the cut.

There is a real signal

1690 kHz, unlicensed and legal at very low power, transmitting over roughly one block of Salt Lake City. Almost nobody will ever hear it that way. That is not a distribution plan. It is a fact about the work, and the reason this is a station and not a podcast wearing a costume.

Everything also arrives here, and by feed, for anyone outside the block.
